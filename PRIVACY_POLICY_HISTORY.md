# PongTrace Privacy Policy History

The public web policy is the complete policy submitted through App Store Connect. The in-app
policy is a shorter summary of the same data practices and links to the public web policy.

## 1.2 — Effective August 30, 2026

- Git snapshot: `2fbf67bf366cfe3acce8f287ebf0e09b070cd3b1`
- Expanded the public policy into a complete 13-section policy in all seven supported languages.
- Kept the in-app policy as a concise summary of the same data practices.
- Consolidated user-directed sharing, email feedback, external services and cross-border transfer
  explanations without naming implementation libraries.
- Removed the dedicated privacy-contact section and visible email address from the policy. Contact
  and feedback remain available from the top level of PongTrace Settings.
- Added build verification that requires policy version 1.2 and rejects obsolete technical or
  contact details in generated policy pages.
